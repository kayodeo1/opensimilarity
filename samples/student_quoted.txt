A Short Note on Scheduling Water in Halls

Scheduling is cheaper than construction, and it is the first thing a warden should try. One published survey puts the point directly: "A hostel block housing four hundred students consumed about thirty thousand litres on a typical weekday during the survey period, with the sharpest peak between six and eight in the morning." I take that finding to mean that any timetable has to be built around the early morning rush rather than around the convenience of the maintenance crew.

Residents respond well when they are told in advance what will happen. A printed notice and a short message to the class representatives cost almost nothing, yet they prevent most of the arguments that otherwise break out at the taps.
