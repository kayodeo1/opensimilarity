CVE 401 Coursework 2: Water Supply in Student Hostels

Question 1. Describe the main sources of water supply for university hostels in your state and explain how reliable each one is during the dry season.

In Ogun State most private hostels sink a single shallow well, while university halls share deeper boreholes. Shallow wells often fail completely by February, whereas the deeper boreholes keep working but deliver less.

Question 2. Propose one low cost change that a hostel administrator could make within a single academic session, and explain how its effect would be measured.

A rota for opening and closing the main valve would stop tanks overflowing at night. Success would show up as fewer complaints logged at the porter's desk and a lower monthly diesel bill for the pump generator.
