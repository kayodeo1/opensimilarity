CVE 401 Coursework 2: Water Supply in Student Hostels

Question 1. Describe the main sources of water supply for university hostels in your state and explain how reliable each one is during the dry season.

Our campus relies on three boreholes and a municipal connection that has not delivered water since 2019. In the harmattan the borehole yields drop noticeably and the pumps trip whenever the voltage sags.

Question 2. Propose one low cost change that a hostel administrator could make within a single academic session, and explain how its effect would be measured.

I would install a float valve alarm on each overhead tank. Its effect can be measured by counting the hours each week in which a block reports no water, before and after installation.
